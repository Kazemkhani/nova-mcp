# Code of conduct

nova-mcp is committed to a welcoming, professional, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and learn from feedback.
- Prioritize the safety of users and contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [amir@amirkazemkhani.com](mailto:amir@amirkazemkhani.com). Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential to the extent practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
